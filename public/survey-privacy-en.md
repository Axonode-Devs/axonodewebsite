# Axonode Survey Privacy Notice

Last Updated : 9.08.2026

## 1. Data Controller
Your personal data is processed by the Axonode Organization in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

Contact us: contact@axonode.org

## 2. What we collect
As a part of this survey, We may collect the following data.
- Contact Information - Your Email Address
- Survey Responses - Survey Responses, Opinions.
- Technical Data - Survey Submission Date, IP Address

## 3. Purpose of collection
We process your personal data for the following purposes:
- To analyze survey answers to create a Personality chart.
- To improve our community and measure the demand for our services.


## 4. Data Transfer and Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Your information is processed securely by the Axonode Organization.

## 5. Data Retention
We will retain your data for as long as the survey is active on our homepage. Once the survey is closed or removed, your personal data (Email and IP address) will be securely deleted or permanently anonymized within 60 days.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR and KVKK, you have the legal right to:

- Learn whether your personal data is being processed and request a copy of it.
- Know if your data is transferred to any third parties domestically or abroad.
- Request the correction of inaccurate data.
- Request the deletion or destruction of your personal data.
